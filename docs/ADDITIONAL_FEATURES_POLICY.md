## Additional Features, Scope Changes & AI Integration Policy

**Document ID:** `NSD-POL-2026-001`  
**Issued By:** Northstack Digital Agency Management  
**Effective Date:** August 2026  
**Applicable To:** All Clients, Projects, and Contracts  

---

### Executive Summary
At **Northstack Digital Agency**, we prioritize delivering projects with high engineering standards, clean architecture, and agreed timelines. 

To maintain delivery schedules, project stability, and cost predictability, this policy establishes clear operational and commercial terms governing any request for **additional features**, **system modifications**, **third-party services**, or **Artificial Intelligence (AI) integrations** introduced after project quotation and contract signing.

---

### 1. Scope Definition & Baseline
- **Baseline Scope:** The features, pages, user flows, and technical deliverables explicitly detailed in the approved **Proposal**, **Statement of Work (SOW)**, or **Service Agreement**.
- **Scope Expansion (Out-of-Scope Work):** Any functionality, design revision, integration, or architectural enhancement not documented in the signed Baseline Scope.

### 3. Special Provisions for Artificial Intelligence (AI) Integrations

AI capabilities introduce unique architectural, technical, and recurring operational considerations:

1. **Development & Engineering Costs:**
   - Prompt engineering, backend orchestration, data sanitization, and fallback safeguards.
   - Vector indexing, context embedding, and database setup.
2. **Third-Party API & Token Charges:**
   - Third-party API charges (e.g., token usage from OpenAI, Gemini, Claude, or Cloud vector hosts) are recurring operational costs billed directly to the client's account or reimbursed via monthly billing.
3. **Model Maintenance & Tuning:**
   - Ongoing fine-tuning, latency optimization, and version updates require an active **Maintenance & Support Retainer**.

---

### 4. Step-by-Step Change Request Workflow

To ensure seamless progress without disrupting the main project timeline:

```
[1. Request Submission] ➔ Client submits request in writing (Email / Portal / WhatsApp)
         │
[2. Technical Impact Analysis] ➔ Northstack assesses feasibility, dependencies & timeline impact
         │
[3. Change Order Quote (CO)] ➔ Northstack issues an itemized addendum with cost & updated delivery date
         │
[4. Client Approval & Sign-Off] ➔ Written sign-off and agreed commitment fee
         │
[5. Sprint Implementation] ➔ Feature scheduled and delivered to staging/production
```

> **Important Note:** Verbal requests during calls or chats are non-binding. Development on any new feature begins only upon formal confirmation of the Change Order.

### 5. Payment Terms for Additional Features
- **Deposit:** 50% commitment deposit required before initiating work on additional feature modules.
- **Completion:** Remaining balance due upon milestone demonstration and before deployment to production.
- **Priority Retainers:** Clients with active Monthly Growth or Maintenance Retainers receive priority turnaround and discounted add-on rates.
